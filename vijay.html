Joseph Vijay's Career and Achievements Joseph Vijay's career in the film industry began as a child artist and he made his acting debut as a leading man in 1992. His career took off with successful films such as 'Poove Unakkaga' (1996) and 'Kadhalukku Mariyadhai' (1997). Vijay transitioned into an action star with 'Thirumalai' (2003) and 'Ghilli' (2004). He became one of the highest-paid actors in India and won numerous accolades, including multiple Tamil Nadu State Film Awards and South Indian International Movie Awards. Wikipedia +1 Vijay's philanthropic efforts began with the founding of the social welfare organization Vijay Makkal Iyakkam (VMI) in 2009. He has been involved in community service, disaster relief efforts, and educational support. His films, such as 'Thuppakki' (2012), 'Kaththi' (2014), 'Theri' (2016), 'Mersal' (2017), 'Sarkar' (2018), 'Bigil' (2019), 'Master' (2021), and 'Leo' (2023), have been commercial successes and have earned him a significant fan following. Wikipedia +1 In February 2024, Vijay announced his retirement from cinema and his formal entry into politics. He launched the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), a secular and egalitarian party. In the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election, TVK secured 108 seats, making it the single-largest party in Tamil Nadu. Vijay was sworn in as Chief Minister on May 10, 2026, marking a historic transition from cinema to state leadership.
